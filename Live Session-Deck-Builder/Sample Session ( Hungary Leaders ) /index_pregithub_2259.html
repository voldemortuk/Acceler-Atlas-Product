Use case · Market Pulse
Know what moved before anyone tells you

Markets move overnight. By the time something reaches your desk as a formal briefing, it's been filtered — the raw signal is gone. You tell it a market and a time window — "Hungary, last 7 days" — and it scans three signal layers, read through your strategic lens.

🏁 External
Competitor pricing, network announcements, exec moves, press — live from the web.
⚖️ Regulatory
Spectrum decisions, competition rulings, consultations — flagged & interpreted.
📧 Internal
What your own team is already flagging in email — before you'd have seen it.
🎯
It asks before it runs. No market + time window? It stops and asks — because "CEE, 30 days" is a different brief from "Hungary, 7 days." The discipline is the value.
Market Pulse · what good looks like
One brief. Four sections.
📄 The brief structure
  • Headline — the single most important thing that moved.
  • What Moved — 3-5 developments: named actor, what, when, cited source.
  • What It Means For Us — tied to your strategic priorities, by number.
  • What To Watch Next — 1-2 things in the next 7-14 days. Early warning.
  • Internal Signals (Stage 2+) — what your team already flagged.
Knowledge sources
  • 🌐 Web Search (live) — the real-time signal layer
  • 📋 Competitive Landscape — Magyar Telekom, 4iG, A1, Vivacom, DIGI
  • ⚖️ Regulatory Watch — spectrum, competition, GDPR
  • 🎯 Strategic Context — your 2026 priorities & open items
  • 📧 Work IQ — Email — internal flags (Stage 2+)
✨
The wow: "What It Means For Us" — the external signal interpreted through your lens, not a generic news summary.
