# Acceler Pre-Sales Discovery Checklist

33 questions across 6 sections. Scoring: each answered = 1, partial = 0.5, missing = 0. Overall % = total / 33.

**Verdicts:** ≥80% Ready to propose · 50-79% Gaps remain — proceed with care · <50% Thin — hold proposal, book follow-up.

## 1. Why They're Here (6)
- [MUST] What's the actual problem they're trying to solve? (not "we need AI training" — what pain triggered this?)
- [MUST] What does success look like 3 months out? (a number: hours saved, adoption %, people certified)
- [MUST] Who decides — and who pays? (buyer / champion / end-users)
- [SHOULD] Have they tried AI training before? What worked / flopped?
- [NICE] Pilot or full rollout?
- [SHOULD] Internal champion — who pushes this when we're not in the room?

## 2. Who's in the Room (5)
- [MUST] What do participants actually do day-to-day? (not job titles)
- [MUST] How many people, how many batches?
- [SHOULD] Technical or non-technical?
- [SHOULD] Current AI usage? (never / dabbling / Copilot daily)
- [NICE] One function or mixed cohort?

## 3. When & How (5)
- [MUST] Virtual or in-person? Where are the people? (time zones)
- [MUST] How much time? Half-day / 1d / 2d / more?
- [SHOULD] When do they want to start? Hard deadline?
- [NICE] Language — English only or more?
- [NICE] Event or moment to tie this to?

## 4. Tools & Access (6)
- [MUST] What AI tools do their people have access to today? (ChatGPT/Copilot/Claude — free/enterprise/none)
- [MUST] Is IT blocking anything? Security restrictions on AI?
- [SHOULD] Teach on their tools or ours? Who provides licences?
- [SHOULD] Hands-on labs / sandboxes needed?
- [NICE] LMS? (Cornerstone, Docebo, SAP)
- [NICE] Chat tool for the cohort? (Slack, Teams, WhatsApp)

## 5. What They Want Built (5)
- [MUST] What should participants be able to DO after? (specific task, not "understand AI")
- [SHOULD] Industry-specific exercises?
- [SHOULD] Final project / capstone — real or simulated?
- [SHOULD] How do they want to measure it worked? (pre/post quiz · manager feedback · 30/60/90 adoption)
- [NICE] Any data they CAN'T use in exercises? (regulated)

## 6. Money & Timeline (5)
- [MUST] Budget range?
- [MUST] When do they decide? Who approves?
- [SHOULD] Talking to other vendors? What are they comparing?
- [NICE] Procurement / legal review — how long?
- [NICE] Certificates — standard Acceler or co-branded?
